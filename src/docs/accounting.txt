The Accounting service is the immutable, double-entry bookkeeping system for your entire platform. Its API needs to be robust, precise, and secure.

  The endpoints revolve around three core concepts:
   1. Ledgers: High-level buckets for accounts (e.g., ASSETS, LIABILITIES, EQUITY).
   2. Accounts: The specific accounts where money is tracked (e.g., "Cash on Hand," "Customer Deposits," "Loan Portfolio").
   3. Journal Entries: The immutable records of transactions, composed of balanced debits and credits.

  Here are the essential endpoints for the Accounting service.

  ---
  A Critical Note: Every single request to this service must include the X-Tenant-Identifier header to specify which institution's books are being affected. The service logic should be built around this, ensuring absolute
  data isolation.
  ---

  1. Ledger Management

  Ledgers are the top-level components of a Chart of Accounts.

  POST /accounting/v1/ledgers
  Creates a new top-level ledger or a sub-ledger.
   * Request Body:

   1     {
   2       "identifier": "1000",
   3       "type": "ASSET",
   4       "name": "Assets",
   5       "parentLedgerIdentifier": null,
   6       "showAccountsInChart": true
   7     }
   * Response: 202 Accepted

  GET /accounting/v1/ledgers
  Fetches all ledgers for the tenant.
   * Response: 200 OK with a list of ledger objects.

  GET /accounting/v1/ledgers/{identifier}
  Fetches a single ledger and its sub-ledgers.
   * Response: 200 OK with the ledger object.

  PUT /accounting/v1/ledgers/{identifier}
  Modifies an existing ledger.
   * Response: 202 Accepted

  ---

  2. Account Management

  Accounts are the leaves of the Chart of Accounts tree where transactions are actually posted.

  POST /accounting/v1/accounts
  Creates a new account within a ledger.
   * Request Body:

   1     {
   2       "identifier": "1110",
   3       "type": "ASSET",
   4       "name": "Cash on Hand",
   5       "ledgerIdentifier": "1000",
   6       "state": "OPEN",
   7       "balance": "0.00"
   8     }
   * Response: 202 Accepted

  GET /accounting/v1/accounts
  Fetches all accounts for the tenant.
   * Response: 200 OK with a list of account objects.

  GET /accounting/v1/accounts/{identifier}
  Fetches a single account, including its current balance.
   * Response: 200 OK with the account object.

  PUT /accounting/v1/accounts/{identifier}
  Modifies an account (e.g., to change its name or state). You cannot change the balance via this endpoint.
   * Response: 202 Accepted

  ---

  3. Transaction Management (The Core Endpoint)

  This is the most important and frequently used endpoint. It records a financial event.

  POST /accounting/v1/transactions
  Posts a new journal entry. The service must validate that the sum of debits equals the sum of credits before accepting the transaction.
   * Request Body:

    1     {
    2       "transactionIdentifier": "unique_id_from_caller_for_idempotency",
    3       "transactionType": "DEPOSIT",
    4       "transactionDate": "2023-10-27T10:30:00Z",
    5       "note": "Customer deposit for account #SAV-001",
    6       "debits": [
    7         {
    8           "accountIdentifier": "1110", // Cash on Hand
    9           "amount": "100.00"
   10         }
   11       ],
   12       "credits": [
   13         {
   14           "accountIdentifier": "2110", // Customer Deposits
   15           "amount": "100.00"
   16         }
   17       ]
   18     }
   * Responses:
       * 202 Accepted: The journal entry is valid and has been posted.
       * 400 Bad Request: The transaction is invalid (e.g., debits do not equal credits, or an account identifier is invalid). The response body must contain a clear error message.
       * 409 Conflict: A transaction with the same transactionIdentifier has already been posted.

  GET /accounting/v1/transactions/{transactionIdentifier}
  Retrieves a specific journal entry by its unique identifier.
   * Response: 200 OK with the full journal entry object.

  ---

  4. Reporting

  GET /accounting/v1/trialbalance
  Generates a Trial Balance report for the tenant. This is a fundamental accounting report.
   * Response: 200 OK with a list of all accounts and their final debit or credit balances.

    1     {
    2       "date": "2023-10-27T23:59:59Z",
    3       "entries": [
    4         {
    5           "accountIdentifier": "1110",
    6           "accountName": "Cash on Hand",
    7           "type": "ASSET",
    8           "balance": "15000.00",
    9           "side": "DEBIT"
   10         },
   11         {
   12           "accountIdentifier": "2110",
   13           "accountName": "Customer Deposits",
   14           "balance": "15000.00",
   15           "side": "CREDIT"
   16         }
   17       ]
   18     }

  These endpoints provide a solid foundation for a robust, auditable, and reliable Accounting microservice.
